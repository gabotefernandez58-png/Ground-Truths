Ground Truths

Introduction

A website project to help everyday consumers and the agricultural community or farmers to help consumers better understand what farmers go through everyday. Most of the time people only get to interact with agriculture at the grocery store, only a few understand the daily operational, financial, and environmental challenges farmers face to produce that food or agricultural produces.

What This Website covers

1. The Reality of Daily Operations of Farmers

Explore the labor, working hours, planning, and practical skills involved in managing a farm.

  • Preparing land and managing crops.
  • Planting, maintaining, and harvesting agricultural products.
  • Physical demands and time commitment for our Farmers.
  • The Equipment's and resources required for farming.

Read more about Daily Operations

2. Uncontrollable Variables and Agricultural Challenges

Explore external factors that farmers cannot always control and how these affect their operations.

  • Weather conditions, typhoons, floods, and droughts.
  • Changing market prices and production expenses.
  • Pests and crop diseases.
  • Transportation and supply chain difficulties.

Read more about Agricultural Challenges

3. Sustainability and Long-Term Agricultural Operations

Explore how farmers maintain agricultural operations despite changing conditions, rising expenses, and production risks.

  • Managing limited resources.
  • Maintaining soil and crop productivity.
  • Adapting to environmental and economic challenges.
  • Maintaining agricultural production over time.

Read more about Sustainability

Our Goal

To build understanding of modern agriculture, give consumers empathy for the people who feed us, and appreciate the effort required to maintain a stable food supply.
